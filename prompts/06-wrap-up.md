# 06 - Wrap-up summary

**When:** Last 5-7 minutes, before you walk the interviewer through it.

```text
Summarize this work for a reviewer in under 150 words:

1. Root cause, in one or two sentences.
2. The fix, and why it is the smallest correct change.
3. Tests added and what each one proves.
4. Exactly which commands were run and their results.
5. What was not verified, and any follow-up worth filing.

Then show `git status` and `git diff --stat`. Be factual; do not overstate
what was tested.
```
